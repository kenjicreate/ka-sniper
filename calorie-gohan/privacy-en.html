Calorie Diet Log – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

日本語

Information this app collects

The app itself does not collect personal information such as your name, email address or contacts. There is no account sign-up.

What is stored on your device

The following information is stored only on your device and is never sent anywhere. It is deleted when you delete the app.

  • Meal records (dish name, estimated calories and salt, portion, time eaten)
  • Weight, water, height, calorie goal, salt and water guides
  • Settings such as your buddy character, text size, language and reminder times
  • Names and relationships (family, care manager, etc.) of people you choose to send summaries to

Photos

Photos you take or choose are used only on your device to find out what the dish is. They are never sent anywhere, and the photos themselves are not saved.

Camera and photo access

The app uses the camera and your photos only when you take or choose a meal photo.

Sharing summaries

“Send to family or carer” passes the summary text to your phone's share feature (email, messages, etc.) only when you tap “Send”. The app never sends anything automatically.

Reminders

Mealtime reminders are scheduled and shown on your device. No external server is used.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show ads, AdMob may use information from your device (such as ad views and interactions, IP address as an approximate location, and crash information). The app does not pass your meal or weight records, or any personal information such as your name, to AdMob. The app does not ask for permission to track you across apps.

For how Google handles information, please see:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google Privacy Policy

About the calorie figures

Calories and salt shown from photos or the food list are estimates only. If your doctor has given you dietary advice, please follow it first.
